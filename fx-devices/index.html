02 Modulation

Modulate anything
in two moves.

Right-click a modulator. Right-drag the knob you want it to move, and how far you drag sets the range. That's all. Every knob then shows a coloured ring for each modulator on it, so you always see what's moving it.

Watch, then try it yourself

  1. 1

    Right-click a modulator

    It blinks, and every knob it can drive lights up in its colour. On a phone, tap it.

  2. 2

    Right-drag a knob

    Up for more, down for a negative range. Hold Alt to make it go both ways.

  3. 3

    That's it

    The ring shows the range and the white dot shows what you hear. Press Esc when you're done.

∿

03 Signal chain

A signal chain
you can actually read.

REAPER's FX window squeezes your chain into a narrow list and shows one plugin at a time. Containers and parallel chains become an indented list that's hard to follow and wastes most of the screen, so they rarely get used. FX Devices draws them the way you'd sketch them on paper.

☰

Parallel FX as tabs

Branches sit side by side as tabs, each with its own mix bar, mute and solo. Open one to see it in full.

▣

Containers as boxes

Group FX into a container, fold it into one device, and give it macros that drive everything inside.

↧

Always know where you are

The chain marks which branch or band you're editing, so you never lose your place.

04 Splitters

Split the signal.
Tweak each part.

Custom splitters cut a sound into parts you can process on their own: low, mid and high bands, mid and side, or the attack and the tail. Drop FX on any part and hear it right away. No pin matrix, no routing tricks.

≋

Band split

Drag the crossovers, pick the slope or linear phase, and give each band its own FX.

◐

Mid-Side

Widen the sides without touching the centre, or clean up the centre on its own.

◭

Transient-Sustain

Shape the attack and the tail separately, so you can add punch without touching the room.

⧉

Nest them

Put a splitter inside a band or a branch. Mute and solo each part to hear what it does.

Questions

Do I need FabFilter plugins?

No. Any plugin works. Plugins without a hand-built panel show their parameters as knobs, and you choose which ones with Learn or the layout editor.

Does it change how my plugins sound?

No. Your plugins do the processing as always. FX Devices gives them a front panel and adds small helper effects for modulation and splitting.

How do I assign a modulator?

Right-click the modulator to arm it, then right-drag any knob: up for a positive range, down for a negative one, and hold Alt to make it go both ways. Press Esc when you're done.

Can I modulate plugins that don't have a custom panel?

Yes. Any parameter you put on a device can take modulators, on any plugin.

Is it the same license as Vertical FX List?

No. FX Devices is its own product with its own license key.

How do I update?

Run the Cool Reaper Script Manager again and it updates FX Devices for you. Your shortcuts, colours and favourites are kept.
