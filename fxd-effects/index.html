05 FAQ

Questions

Do I need FX Devices?

No. The effects are plugins: add them to any track and use their own windows. If you also run FX Devices, each one gets its full device layout there too.

I already own FX Devices. Do I need this?

No. An FX Devices license or trial unlocks every effect and Prism EQ+, as before.

Which DAWs and systems?

REAPER 7 on macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows. The license is checked through REAPER, so other hosts aren't supported yet.

What happens without a license?

The effects load and pass the audio through unchanged, and their window shows how to start the free trial or sign in.

Can I move up to FX Devices later?

Yes, buy FX Devices whenever you like. Your effects keep working on either license.
