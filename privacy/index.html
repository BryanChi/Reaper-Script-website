Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Cool Reaper Scripts (coolreaperscripts.com) is run by Bryan Chi. This page explains what we collect when you use the website, sign in, buy a license or use the scripts (Vertical FX List, FX Devices and the Cool Reaper Script Manager), and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us; we use the email to find your licenses.
  • Licenses and trials: which scripts you own or are trying, your license keys, and when they were created or expire.
  • Activated computers: when a script activates a computer, it sends a device ID made from that computer's identifiers, so your license can count the computers it's used on (3 per license).
  • Purchases: payments are handled by PayPal. We receive the order details and your email; we never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Feedback you send: if you send feedback or a bug report from a script, we receive what you write and anything you attach (such as a screenshot, a recording or a log).

How we use it

  • To sign you in, deliver and check your licenses and trials, and activate your computers.
  • To send emails about your purchase, trial or license (sign-in links, receipts, keys).
  • To answer support requests and fix bugs.

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use it for anything else. Google user data is used only to sign you in, as described above.

Who handles it for us

Your data is stored and processed by the services the site runs on: Supabase (accounts and license database), Vercel (website hosting), PayPal (payments), Resend (email) and Google (sign in with Google). They handle it only to provide those services.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and license records for as long as you have an account, so your licenses keep working. To see, correct or delete your data, email brwchi@gmail.com and we'll do it. Deleting your account also ends your licenses.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
